# Resuvika Omniball — privacy policy draft

Publisher: Vikashya Resuvika Private Limited. Effective date, support contact, public URL, applicable publisher address and any jurisdiction-specific requirements must be filled in by the publisher before publication. This draft describes this implementation; update it if you add analytics, accounts, advertising, cloud storage or other SDKs.

Resuvika Omniball saves whiteboard drawings, class notes, preferences, named boards and trial timing on your device. The standalone whiteboard does not automatically upload drawing or note content. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove these files. You can export a board backup or PNG using your device’s share sheet; the destination you select receives that exported content.

Google Play or Apple handles the purchase payment. The app does not receive your full card details. RevenueCat processes store purchase/receipt history, purchase status and an anonymous app user identifier to verify access, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide the publisher’s sales/subscription reporting. It receives relevant technical/store information needed for its SDK operation. Network providers/store services also receive ordinary network connection information. Refer to [RevenueCat’s privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and your store’s privacy information for their handling of this data.

There is no advertising SDK, contact collection, microphone recording or camera capture in this build. The app’s recording view only hides menus and optionally branding for your own device screen recorder.

On Android, optional floating annotation uses Display over other apps permission and a foreground service only after you start it. It records your own pen/shape/text input on Omniball’s transparent drawing layer and stores that ink draft privately on your device. It does not read another app’s screen pixels, text fields, Accessibility events or audio. A visible floating control and ongoing service notification provide stop/return actions. The user can revoke the overlay permission in Android settings. Protected apps/system screens may suppress overlays. No automatic background or boot start is included.

Optional laptop pairing opens a local companion in your browser using a pairing link. Drawings/notes created in that companion are exchanged with and may be saved on the computer running Resuvika Omniball. Use a network and computer you trust. This companion is separate from the app’s offline board.

You can delete local app data using device settings. Purchases remain associated with your store account and the purchase-verification service as needed for access/restore and their retention obligations. The publisher must define and disclose its final retention/request process before publishing this policy.
